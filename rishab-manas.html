Rogue Robotics 98721F · Team profile

Rishab Manas

Rishab Manas is one of two captains of Rogue Robotics 98721F, alongside Colin Southard. He leads the software division and handles team logistics for our student-led VEX V5 team in Fremont, California.

In his own words

Software, hardware decisions, and the work behind the team

Rishab’s responsibilities connect the code that controls our robot with the logistics that support the team. His role includes finances and fundraising, major hardware decisions, autonomous programming, and driver controls.

I handle logistics for the team, such as finances and fundraising. I also lead major hardware decisions, and run the software division, writing our autonomous routines and driver controls.

Rishab Manas, Captain · Rogue Robotics 98721F

Responsibilities

What Rishab works on

Software
Rishab runs the software division, writing the robot’s autonomous routines and driver controls. In VEX V5 matches, autonomous code controls the robot for the first fifteen seconds before the driver takes over.
Hardware decisions
Rishab leads major hardware decisions. Fellow captain Colin Southard coordinates timelines, helps guide design decisions, and prototypes and tests mechanisms.
Team logistics
Rishab handles the team’s finances and fundraising. Rogue Robotics is fully student-run, with students responsible for the robot, engineering notebook, budgets, and workshops.
