About Rogue Robotics

Our Mission

Rogue Robotics is a student-led VEX V5 team focused on engineering, teamwork, and growth. We build competitive robots while creating opportunities for younger students to learn design, coding, strategy, and leadership.

Our History

Rogue Robotics was built by students who wanted to compete, improve, and create something meaningful together. Through every build season, scrimmage, and event, our team has kept growing in skill, confidence, and ambition.

We continue to push ourselves in VEX V5 while also giving back through outreach and support for younger students interested in robotics.
