What is VEX V5?

VEX V5 is an international competition where student teams build, code, and drive custom robots to win a new game challenge every season. It’s all about taking an idea, bringing it to life, and competing head-to-head on the field in a 2v2 match. What we actually do in VEX: Design & Build: Brainstorming, CADing, and building different prototypes and mechanisms. Coding: Writing autonomous code for the robot to run by itself, plus easy driver controls in the match. Strategy: Adapting on the spot and working with alliance partners during matches.pare our robot to handle real match challenges.
