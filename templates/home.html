Who We Are

Our team collaborates across design, build, programming, and driving to create robots that improve every match. We’re based in Fremont and compete in VEX V5.

About Rogue Robotics

What is VEX V5?

VEX V5 is a robotics competition where teams design, build, and program robots to play a new game every season. Students learn engineering, coding, strategy, and teamwork while competing at local and regional events.

Rogue Robotics uses the VEX V5 system to prototype mechanisms, tune code, and prepare our robot to handle real match challenges.
