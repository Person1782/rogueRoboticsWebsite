Sharing robotics with our community

Rogue Robotics believes robotics should reach beyond competition. Through presentations, workshops, and hands-on learning, we work to inspire younger students and make STEM more accessible.

Thornton Middle School Presentation

We presented at Thornton Middle School to introduce students to robotics, teamwork, and engineering. Our team shared what VEX V5 is, how competitions work, and what it is like to design, build, and program a robot.

>Introduced students to VEX V5 robotics. >Shared our experience as a student-led team. >Encouraged younger students to explore STEM.

Summer Camp

We held a summer camp to give students a more hands-on robotics experience. The camp focused on learning, teamwork, and problem-solving through engaging STEM activities.

>Created a hands-on robotics learning environment. >Helped students build confidence with STEM concepts. >Made engineering more accessible and fun.
