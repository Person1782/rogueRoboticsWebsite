Sponsor Us

We have no school funding and no adult mentors. Community support is what keeps this team on the field.

Why it matters

Every dollar goes straight into the robot or the community

Rogue Robotics is a student-led VEX V5 team that relies on outside support to fund robot parts, tools, competition expenses, and outreach efforts. We manage the budget ourselves, and we can tell you exactly where support goes.

We are currently looking for sponsors who want to back student engineering, robotics education, and STEM access in the Bay Area.

Where support goes

Four things sponsorship pays for

Robot parts
Motors, gears, wheels, metal, pneumatics, and the spares that keep us running through a full tournament day.
Tools & workspace
Drills, bits, taps, hardware, and field elements so we can build and practice properly between competitions.
Competition costs
Tournament registration, team registration, and the travel that comes with events outside Fremont.
Outreach
Free showcases and summer camps for local students - materials, kits, and hardware they get to use hands-on.
