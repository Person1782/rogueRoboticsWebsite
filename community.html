Outreach

Community

Robotics shouldn’t stop at competition. We run showcases and camps so other students can start where we did.

Sharing robotics with our community

Through presentations, workshops, and hands-on builds, we work to make STEM reachable for students who don’t have a program yet.

Event 01 · Thornton Middle School

A showcase on how to run your own team

We presented at Thornton Middle School to introduce students to robotics, teamwork, and engineering. The team explained what VEX V5 is, how competitions work, and what it takes to design, build, and program a robot — without a coach.

Introduced
VEX V5 robotics, match format, and the season game.
Shared
What running a fully student-led team actually looks like.
Encouraged
Younger students to start exploring STEM on their own terms.

Thornton Middle School showcase.

Event 02 · Summer camp

A week of building, coding, and documenting

Our STEM summer camp gave local kids a hands-on robotics experience: assembling real VEX hardware, programming it, and writing down what they tried. The focus was learning through problem-solving rather than following instructions.

Built
A hands-on robotics learning environment with real competition parts.
Grew
Camper confidence with mechanical and programming concepts.
Made
Engineering feel accessible and fun for first-timers.

Summer camp build session.
